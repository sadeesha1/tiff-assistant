Tiff privacy policy

Tiff is a personal assistant used by its developer only, for his own accounts. This page explains how it handles Google user data.

What Tiff accesses

Only the developer's own Google account, and only with the permissions he granted:

  • Gmail: read his messages and write drafts. A message is sent only after he confirms it.
  • Google Calendar: read and manage events on his calendar.
  • Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms: only files Tiff itself creates (the drive.file permission). Tiff cannot see his other Drive files.
  • YouTube: read-only access to his playlists, subscriptions and liked videos, and search.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to answer his own requests: for example summarising his unread email, or creating a Doc he asked for. To understand a request, the relevant text may be processed by the AI model provider he has chosen, under that provider's no-training terms. It is never used to train general AI models, never used for advertising, and never sold or shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept

Sign-in tokens are stored in the operating system's credential store on his own computer. Tiff keeps no copy of email or Drive content beyond what a request needs, and stores nothing on any server of its own.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tiff's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Tiff also revokes and deletes its tokens when it is disconnected.
