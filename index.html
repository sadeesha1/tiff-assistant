Tiff

Tiff is a private, personal assistant built and used by one person for himself. It runs on his own computer and talks to him through his own Telegram chat.

With his permission, Tiff can help him with his own Google account: reading and drafting his email, managing his calendar, creating Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms for him, and listing his own YouTube playlists and subscriptions.

Tiff is not a public product. It isn't offered to anyone else, and nobody else's data is collected.
